📋 Collection rules

⏰Put your bin out by 6:30am on collection day (from 6pm the evening before).
🔢Mark your bin with your house number.
🚫Lid must be fully closed — no side waste or overfilling.
🏠Retrieve your bin by midnight on collection day.

Which bin is which?
Green Bin
Fortnightly
✓ Accepted
Grass, leaves, twigs, small branches, bread, egg shells, dairy, meat, fish, fruit, veg, teabags, leftover food. No bag needed.
✗ Not accepted
Cardboard, soil, rubble, large branches, plastic bags, liquid oils/fat, dog foul, cat litter
Brown Bin
3-weekly
✓ Accepted
Cans, tins, aerosols, glass bottles & jars, plastic bottles, yoghurt pots, plastic pots & tubs, aluminium foil
✗ Not accepted
Plastic bags/film, food, nappies, electrical items, hosepipes, wine glasses, window glass, Tetra Pak, coffee pods
Blue Bin / White Sack
3-weekly
✓ Accepted
Office paper, junk mail, catalogues, newspapers, magazines, envelopes, greeting cards (no glitter), cardboard, toilet/kitchen roll tubes
✗ Not accepted
Nappies, food, plastic bags/wrappers/trays, polystyrene, books, crisp packets, Tetra Pak, wallpaper
Black Bin
3-weekly
✓ Accepted
Anything not recyclable elsewhere — nappies, incontinence pads, dog foul, cat litter
✗ Not accepted
Soil, rubble, building materials, liquid waste (paints/oils), hazardous waste, batteries, electrical items
